## Design Context

### Users
Primary audience: recruiters reviewing Kaung Si Thu for interviews.

They are likely scanning quickly across desktop and mobile during hiring workflows, so the site needs to communicate credibility, clarity, and polish within a short attention window. Their main job is to understand fit fast: who he is, what he has built, what experience he has, and how to contact him for an interview.

### Brand Personality
Voice and tone should feel attractive, focused, and minimal.

The emotional goal is to create immediate interest without noise: recruiters should feel that the work is polished, professional, and easy to trust. The interface should help move the viewer toward interview intent rather than decorative browsing.

### Aesthetic Direction
Direction: refined minimalism with enough visual distinction to feel memorable, but never busy.

The experience should support both light and dark themes. Since no direct references or anti-references were provided, future design work should avoid generic portfolio tropes and instead emphasize strong typography, disciplined spacing, selective accent use, and a fast-reading structure tuned for recruiter evaluation.

### Design Principles
1. Optimize for recruiter scanning speed: surface identity, role, experience, projects, and contact paths immediately.
2. Keep the interface minimal but not bland: use restraint, then spend detail where it increases memorability.
3. Make polish signal competence: typography, spacing, hierarchy, and motion should imply engineering care.
4. Preserve clarity across both light and dark themes without turning either mode into a novelty.
5. Treat every section as interview support: remove anything that distracts from credibility, fit, or next-step contact.
